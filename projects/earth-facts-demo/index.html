A few facts about the world we live in

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the terrestrial planets in our solar system. It is the only planet in our solar system not named after a Greek or Roman god. Earth formed roughly 4.54 billion years ago and remains the only known planet to support life.

About the earth

  • Polar Diameter: 12,714 km
  • Mass: 5.97 × 10^24 kg
  • Moons: 1 (The Moon)

Top 3 facts about the earth

  1. The Earth's rotation is gradually slowing.
  2. The Earth was once believed to be the centre of the universe.
  3. Earth has a powerful magnetic field.
